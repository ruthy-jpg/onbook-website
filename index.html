How it works

Four steps, run for you every day.

You describe what you sell once. Onbook does the searching, the writing and the sending, and hands you the replies and the booked calls.

01

Find

Onbook watches for the moments companies start to buy: a raise, a hire, a launch. Each day it picks the ones that fit what you sell, from 30,000+ companies.

Signals: a raise, a hire, a launch
02

Write

For each company it picks the right people and writes a first message that names the reason. It sends from your own LinkedIn, X and email, in small daily batches.

Your accounts, a safe daily number
03

Stop

The moment anyone at a company replies or books, everyone else there is stopped, on every channel. Nobody gets a second message from you.

One reply stops the whole company
04

Book

Replies and booked calls land with you. You open your calendar and the calls are there.

Replies and calls, nothing to manage
Why Onbook
